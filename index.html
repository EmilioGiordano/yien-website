One terminal, or four.

A small fix gets one agent. A rewrite gets four. They're real terminals, so type to any agent, or open a shell right next to it. Whatever you can't see keeps running.

The panel on the right follows the terminal you're in. Everything Git lives there, one click from the code, and whatever you open lands in a tab next to your terminals.

Bring the agents you already use.

YIEN isn't another agent. It runs the command-line tools you already have, with your own accounts, in real terminals. If it runs in a shell, it runs here.
